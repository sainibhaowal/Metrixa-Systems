# Code of Conduct

Metrixa contributors are expected to make participation respectful, clear,
and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete technical issues.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Protect private, customer, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
conduct, deliberate exposure of secrets or personal data, and disruptive
behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainer through the contact method
listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and may lead
to a warning, removal of content, or loss of participation privileges.

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone represents the
project publicly.
